Hunting explosive gaps, not smooth trends. Does a big gap keep running, and what does the day look like?
How to read this. The daily table asks: when this stock gapped up by X%, how often did it keep running that day (not fade)? The hi-vol column is the one that matters — a gap on 2x+ normal volume is a real ignition; a quiet gap is a trap. Float is the fuel: under ~75M shares (amber) is where violent moves live. The intraday table shows the SHAPE of the biggest recent gaps — did they run and hold, or spike and fade? That tells you whether these are catchable or whether they trap you at the top.
